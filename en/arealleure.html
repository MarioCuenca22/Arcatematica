Leisure area

ARCA TEMÀTICA offers and designs services and activities for fun and entertainment, always with a pedagogical foundation to our work.

We adapt to the age and interests of each group, from children and young people to older people, and we can take care of anything from a one-off activity to running a whole venue.

Children and young people

  • Day camps

    During the school holidays, with games, workshops and outings.

  • Themed day camps

    With a common thread that gives meaning to every activity.

  • Intensive sessions

    One or a few days devoted to a specific topic.

  • Themed residential camps

    Stays away from home to live together and learn through play.

Older people and tourism

  • Older people

    Activities designed around their pace and interests.

  • Tourist activities

    Routes and proposals to discover a place in a different way.

Organisation and management

  • Recreational and educational set-ups

    Events, fairs and activities for organisations and city councils.

  • Management of cultural and leisure centres

    Programming and staff to run a facility.
