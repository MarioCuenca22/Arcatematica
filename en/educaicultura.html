Education and culture

This is ARCA TEMÀTICA's main line of work. We prepare activities to discover heritage, history and the local environment, designed for people of all ages.

Each activity is adapted to the age, knowledge and needs of the group, whether it is a school, an organisation, a city council or a group of families.

Workshops and activities

  • Educational workshops

    To learn by observing, touching and experimenting.

  • After-school activities

    Ongoing programmes throughout the school year.

  • Themed day camps

    Holiday camps with a common thread.

  • Tailor-made activities

    When what you need doesn't fit any existing proposal.

Tours

  • Guided tours

    Of museums, heritage sites and town centres.

  • Theatrical guided tours

    With characters who tell the story in the first person.

Materials and training

  • Teaching materials

    Worksheets and resources for use in the classroom.

  • Information and outreach materials

    Leaflets, panels and content to explain a place.

  • Educational and cultural campaigns

    Programmes for institutions and city councils.

  • Training for educators

    Sessions for teachers and youth leaders.
