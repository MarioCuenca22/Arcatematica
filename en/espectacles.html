Shows

ARCA TEMÀTICA's Shows Area is made up of musicians, film and theatre actors and directors, mime artists and circus performers.

The wide range of artists we work with lets us create shows in very different styles, adapted to the audience and the venue.

Tailor-made shows

We build the show around what you need: a party, an opening, an event or a celebration. We choose the format and the artists that fit it.

Storytelling

Stories told aloud, with music, objects or puppets, for children and families. They can take place in libraries, schools or outdoors.

Theatrical tours

Characters in costume who tell the story of a place in the first person. You'll find more information in Education and culture.
